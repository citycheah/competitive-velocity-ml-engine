🏛️ Strategic Paradigm & Architectural Decisions

💡 Why an "Informative Prior Engine"? (The Low-SNR Paradigm)

Predicting viral breakouts at publication moment ($t_0$) is inherently noisy (base breakout rate ~13–15%). Chasing unrealistic raw accuracy leads to catastrophic false positives and user alert fatigue. Instead, this system frames $t_0$ velocity as a calibrated decision support prior:

  • Normalized Target: Removes subscriber scale confounding using a channel-specific rolling 30-day $Z$-score.
  • Zero Look-Ahead Bias: Strictly terminates all feature aggregation at 1 PRECEDING (before publication).
  • Operational Precision Floor: Calibrates probabilities and enforces an alert threshold ($\tau^* = 0.27$) guaranteeing a 40% precision floor and 3.03x lift over base rate.
  • 100% Local-First: Runs entirely offline on DuckDB + Parquet without requiring external cloud accounts or paid APIs.
